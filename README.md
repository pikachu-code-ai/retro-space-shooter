# retro-space-shooter# Retro Space Shooter 🚀

Welcome to **Retro Space Shooter**, an endless, arcade-style top-down shoot 'em up built entirely with HTML5 Canvas, vanilla JavaScript, and Tailwind CSS. Defend the galaxy from endless waves of enemy ships, falling blocks, and laser-spewing barriers!

## 🎮 Play the Game
https://pikachu-code-ai.github.io/retro-space-shooter/

## ✨ Features

* **Endless Arcade Action:** Survive as long as you can. The game gets progressively harder as your score increases and you level up!
* **Multiple Enemy Types:**
  * **Basic Blocks:** Fall straight down.
  * **Spaceships:** Dive straight down and fire lasers directly ahead.
  * **Purple Barriers:** Lock onto a target altitude and aim their lasers directly at your ship!
* **Power-Ups:** Destroy enemies for a chance to drop powerful weapon upgrades:
  * **Rapid Fire (R):** Drastically increases your shooting speed.
  * **Spread Shot (S):** Fires lasers in three different directions at once.
* **Responsive & Cross-Platform:** Play seamlessly on both desktop (keyboard) and mobile (touch) devices.

## 🕹️ Controls

**Desktop:**
* **Move:** `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` or `Arrow Keys`
* **Shoot:** `Spacebar`

**Mobile / Touch:**
* **Move:** Touch and drag your finger across the screen.
* **Shoot:** Auto-fires while touching the screen.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **HTML5 Canvas:** For rendering the retro game world, entities, and particle explosions.
* **Vanilla JavaScript:** Powers the core game loop, physics, collision detection (AABB), and enemy AI.
* **Tailwind CSS:** For styling the retro, pixel-text UI overlays and menus.
